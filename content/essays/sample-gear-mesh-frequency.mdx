If a gear with $N$ teeth turns at $f_{\text{shaft}}$ revolutions per second, its teeth mesh at

$$
f_{\text{mesh}} = N \cdot f_{\text{shaft}}
$$

Sidebands appear at $f_{\text{mesh}} \pm k \cdot f_{\text{shaft}}$ when the shaft wobbles or a tooth is damaged. That is why the shaft frequency is the first thing to pin down from the tachometer: without it, every other feature drifts with engine speed.
